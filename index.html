The Magic Story

Matthew would rush out of school every day and go home as quickly as he could.

Then he would sit down and read "The Magic Story," his favourite Kindle book.

However, the book began to light brightly when he began to read it one day.

Matthew instantly vanished with a WHOOSH. Matthew landed near a castle with a bump.

It looked exactly like the one in the book. He made the decision to visit the castle to see what it has to offer.

He met a girl named Goldilocks along the way who welcomed him in for lunch.

They shared a large bowl of oatmeal and ate till they were satisfied.

When Matthew was completed, he waved goodbye to his friend and returned to the castle.


Try to understand that and you'll see that nothing is of less important as you think.
